The balance from the statement or your bank's app, and the date it applies to. After applying, Budget Buddy checks your ledger against it. Leave it empty to use the statement's own balance, if it has one. For a card, enter the amount you owe.

Nothing you upload is kept. For a CSV, its first few rows are sent to the AI to work out which column is which. Screenshots, and the whole of a PDF statement, are sent to the AI to read the transactions in them. The descriptions of lines you're missing are sent to suggest categories.
