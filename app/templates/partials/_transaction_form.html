Recurring income or bills? Set them up once on the Scheduled page and they post automatically.

Still counts toward your account balance, but is left out of the analytics charts so first-time setup or fix-up entries don't distort your trends.

For a charge whose real amount lands later — a fuel pump that authorises $1.00, a tip, a hotel hold. It stays pinned to the top of your History until you mark it posted, and it counts in every figure in the meantime.
