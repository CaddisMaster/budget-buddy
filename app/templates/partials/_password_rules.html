{# The password rules, written ONCE and included by both /change-password and /admin/create-user (#249, #250). Both routes enforce the same two rules — auth.change_password and admin.create_user — and stating them in two places is how the two copies start to disagree. Expects `field_id`: the id of the password input to watch. ⚠️ THE CAP IS 72 BYTES, NOT 72 CHARACTERS. bcrypt silently truncates past 72 bytes, which is why the routes reject rather than truncate. Two things follow, and both are easy to get wrong: · a `maxlength` attribute cannot express this — it counts UTF-16 code units, so 72 accented characters (144 bytes) sail past it and are then rejected by the server, which is the exact "told only after submitting" behaviour #249 exists to remove. There is deliberately no maxlength. · the counter below measures with TextEncoder, which yields UTF-8 bytes. `value.length` would be wrong for any non-ASCII password. With JS off the rules still render as text; only the live count is lost, and the server is the guard either way. #}

At least 8 characters, and at most 72 bytes. Most characters are one byte; accents and emoji are more.
