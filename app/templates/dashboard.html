What's new in v0.3.0 2026-07-31
Mark a charge that hasn't settled yet
  • Tick "Pending" when you add it: a fuel pump authorises $1.00 and the real amount turns up a day or two later. Tips and hotel holds do the same thing. Now you can say so instead of leaving a placeholder in the ledger.
  • It stays in front of you: a pending transaction is pinned to the top of History with a badge until you press "Mark posted", rather than sinking out of sight under everything you've spent since.
  • It still counts everywhere: your dashboard, budgets and balance all include it, because the money really did leave the account. Fixing the amount later doesn't un-flag it — clearing it is always your call.
The spending chart is easier to read
  • Six categories, plus "Other": the doughnut used to draw a slice for every category you have. Past about six, the slices stop being tellable apart — so the smaller ones are now grouped into a single grey "Other". Hover it to see how many it stands for.
  • Your totals haven't changed: "Other" is exactly the sum of what it replaces, and every other screen — budgets, insights, the CSV export — still shows each category separately.
  • No more twin colours: two categories could previously be given the identical colour, which made the legend useless for telling them apart. Each one now keeps its own, and it stays put when you filter by month.
Small things
  • The CSV export says what each row is: a new Kind column marks transfers and balance adjustments, so a spreadsheet total no longer double-counts money you only moved between your own accounts.
