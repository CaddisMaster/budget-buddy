What's new in v0.7.0 2026-08-17
Bills whose amount changes can now tell you when they post
  • Some bills are the same every month; some are not. A subscription is the same figure each time, but electricity or water lands on a fixed day for a different amount — so the transaction Budget Buddy creates for you arrives carrying last month's number.
  • A schedule can now be marked “the amount changes every time.” It still posts the amount you set up, but once it has, you get a notification telling you to check what it actually came to.
  • Tapping it takes you straight there, to your transactions, where you can correct the amount in place rather than hunting for it.
Pending transactions stay at the top of your history
  • A pending charge is pinned to the top — a fuel pump that authorises a pound, a hotel hold — so you can’t forget to correct it once the real figure lands.
  • That pinning used to apply only to the page you were on. Once you had more than a page of newer transactions, an older pending charge quietly sat at the top of page three instead, which is exactly where you would never look.
  • They now appear together at the top of the first page, and only there, so nothing is listed twice. Your balances are unchanged — a pending amount still counts towards every figure exactly as it did.
